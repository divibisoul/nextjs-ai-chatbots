## Pair wave

`N06×N05` → `N05×N04` → `N04×N03` → `N03×N02` → `N02×N01`

Two compatible adjacent pair fronts may progress simultaneously. Each pair must inspect real agents, tools, capabilities, transport, execution and context and then derive concrete compositional functions. Pair outputs are handed forward through GitHub.

## Fusion levels

**Level 1:** individual nucleus hardening.

**Level 2:** `N1×N2`, `N3×N4`, `N5×N6`.

**Level 3:** cross-pair composition such as `(N1×N2)×(N3×N4)`.

**Level 4:** six-nucleus cooperative composition.

The final “Super GPU” is a distributed execution architecture: inter-nucleus parallelism plus intra-nucleus worker parallelism, with routing, aggregation, validation and recovery.

## Evidence discipline

A commit message containing terms such as `connect`, `fusion`, `federation` or `prefrontal cortex` is evidence of a change having been committed, not proof that runtime interoperability is complete. Each receiving front must inspect the actual files and tests before building on the claim.
